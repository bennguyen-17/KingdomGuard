# Kingdom Guard

## Register
Product. Unity 2D tower defense for Windows, controlled primarily with the mouse.

## Users and purpose
Casual strategy players defend a castle by placing towers and surviving waves. The GDD defines a 10–15 minute session and a smaller 5–6 wave MVP.

## Existing identity
Preserve the current pixel fantasy UI, dark navy surfaces, gold accents and LegacyRuntime font in UIManager. This task adds audio controls, not a visual redesign.

## Design principles
Audio controls remain reachable from menus and gameplay, show separate music and effects percentages, support keyboard navigation, and save settings. Keep gameplay visible when the controls are closed. The GDD and committed UI supply product context.
